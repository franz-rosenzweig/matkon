# Adding recipes

Most of my recipes come in by themselves, from the reels I save on Instagram (see [The browser extension](browser-extension.md)). But a good recipe also turns up on a food blog, in a cookbook on the shelf, or on a card in my grandmother's handwriting, so Matkon takes those too. The **+** at the top of every page opens **Add**.

## A link

Paste a link and tap **Add**.

- **An Instagram reel** goes into the same queue as the reels I save, and comes in the same way. That works even for a reel that isn't in my collection.
- **Any other recipe page** (a blog, a newspaper's food section, a site like that) becomes a Matkon recipe from what the page itself says. Most recipe sites describe their recipes in a standard way that Matkon reads directly; for the rest, it reads the page's text. The photo is the page's own. On the recipe, the byline is the site's name, and **Recipe page** opens the page. A page that isn't a recipe comes in as "Not a recipe", like a reel that turns out to be a cast-iron skillet review.

The same rule holds as for reels: only what the page says. If it leaves an amount out, so does Matkon, and it says so under **Not in the reel**.

## Photos

**Choose photos** takes up to four pictures of one recipe: a cookbook page, both sides of a card, a recipe on a screen someone sent me. On a phone, the picker can also take the picture. Matkon shrinks them before they go up, reads them like it reads a reel, and the first photo becomes the recipe's picture. The byline is whoever added it.

Either way it takes a minute or so, and then the recipe is in the kitchen like any other: it can be planned, cooked, rated, translated if it's in Hebrew, and fixed if Matkon got something wrong (see [Fixing a recipe](recipes.md#fixing-a-recipe)).

## Sharing into Matkon

On Android, Matkon sits in the phone's share menu, the web app installed from Chrome or the [Android app](android-app.md). Share a reel from Instagram, or a recipe page from the browser, pick **Matkon**, and it says **Added**, **Already on the way**, or **Already in Matkon** with the recipe. If Matkon was already on the home screen before it learned this, remove it and install it again so the share menu picks it up. iPhones and iPads can't do this, because Safari doesn't let a web page into the share menu.
